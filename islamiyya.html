About Our Islamiyya

Our Islamiyya programme is open to pupils of Humble Scholars and to children from the wider community in Niger State. Classes focus on the Book of Allah, Arabic literacy and Islamic manners.

Whether your child is a beginner or already memorising, our qualified teachers guide them step by step with patience and care.

  • ☪ Qur'an recitation, memorisation and Tajweed
  • ☪ Arabic reading and writing
  • ☪ Fiqh, Hadith and Islamic manners (Adab)
  • ☪ Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ
  • ☪ Tahfeez classes for committed students
