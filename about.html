About Humble Scholars

A foundation for this life and the Hereafter.

Our Story

Humble Scholars was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that a child can be both academically excellent and deeply rooted in Islamic values. Located in Niger State, our school offers a seamless learning journey from Creche through Kindergarten, Nursery 1–3, Primary 1–6, and Junior Secondary School (JSS1–JSS3, Post Basic 3).

Our name reflects our philosophy. We seek knowledge — rigorous, curious, ambitious learning — but we pursue it with humility, because all knowledge comes from Allah. Every classroom, every lesson and every interaction is designed to shape not just what a child knows, but who a child becomes.

Beyond our daytime academic programme, we run a dedicated Islamiyya section in the evenings and on weekends, open to our pupils and to children across the wider community.

Vision

To be a leading Islamic school in Niger State — raising a generation of humble, God-conscious scholars who excel in both worldly knowledge and the Deen, and who serve their community with integrity and character.

Mission

To provide a balanced, high-quality education that nurtures the intellect, the character and the soul. Through a strong Western academic curriculum integrated with Qur'anic learning, Tajweed, Arabic and Islamic manners, we prepare every child to succeed in this life and the Hereafter.
