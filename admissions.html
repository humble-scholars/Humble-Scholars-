Admissions

Begin your child's journey with us.

Requirements

  • ✓ Completed application form
  • ✓ Birth certificate or age declaration
  • ✓ 4 recent passport photographs
  • ✓ Previous school report (for Primary 1+)
  • ✓ Transfer certificate (for transfers)
  • ✓ Entrance assessment (JSS1–JSS3)

The Process

1. Submit enquiry or visit the school.

2. Collect and complete the application form.

3. Attend the entrance assessment (where applicable).

4. Receive an offer of admission.

5. Pay fees and complete enrolment.
